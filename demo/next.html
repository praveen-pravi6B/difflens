What just happened

One click, two pages, no second tab

The live view runs both pages as iframes inside a single Chromium tab. Clicking a link resolves the element, clicks the same element in the other iframe, and each side navigates on its own origin.

01

Resolve the element

The click point is mapped into the pane, then the element is described by id, test id, aria label, name, href, role and text.

02

Find its twin

The other page is searched with the same descriptor. The first unique match is clicked at its real coordinates.

03

Let both navigate

Each side follows its own href, so local and staging move to the equivalent page without sharing credentials or cookies.
